FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Twitch viewer bot and its AI features.

Is Twitch Viewer Bot AI really free?
Yes. The project is fully open source and free to use. You can grab the latest Windows build directly from the download page — no subscription or license key required.
How many viewers can I inject at once?
The multi-threaded engine scales with your machine and proxy pool. With a healthy proxy list and sufficient threads, you can run thousands of concurrent viewer sessions. Start conservatively and ramp up gradually.
How does the GPT chat responder work?
You supply an API key in the settings panel. The bot reads recent chat context and generates natural replies through the configured GPT model, keeping the conversation flowing while you focus on streaming.
Do I need proxies to use it?
Proxies are strongly recommended. The built-in rotation engine distributes sessions across your proxy pool for stability. Running without proxies works but is much more likely to hit rate limits.
